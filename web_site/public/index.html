Frequently asked questions

How do I back up or sync my data?

PawVault works anonymously out of the box. To back up and sync across devices, sign in from the app with email, Google, or Apple — your existing data is linked to your account automatically.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

PawVault Pro is billed through your App Store account. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone (or App Store → your profile → Subscriptions). Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore a purchase?

If Pro doesn't appear after reinstalling or switching devices, open the paywall in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can delete individual pets, documents, and other records directly in the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email us at chrnknzhnkv@gmail.com and we'll process the request.

Are the AI features a substitute for a vet?

No. PawVault's AI only organizes information you provide into editable drafts that you review and confirm. PawVault is a record-keeping tool and is not a substitute for professional veterinary care. Always consult a qualified veterinarian for health decisions.

Privacy Policy

Effective June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PawVault ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the PawVault mobile app (the "App").

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in, we process your email address and basic profile details from your chosen provider (email, Google, or Apple). You can use the App anonymously without an account.
  • Pet and health records you enter. Pet profiles, timeline events, documents and images you upload, reminders, and notes.
  • Purchase information. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Aggregated, non-identifying analytics about how features are used, to improve the App.

How we use information

  • To provide, sync, and back up your pet records.
  • To enable AI features you choose to use (see below).
  • To operate subscriptions and to improve the App.

AI features

When you use Smart Input or document scanning, the text or image you choose to process is sent to Google's Gemini models to structure it into a draft that you review and confirm. We do not send this content for AI processing unless you initiate the feature.

Service providers

We share information only as needed with providers that operate the App on our behalf: Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, analytics), Google (Gemini/Vertex AI for the AI features), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Apple / Google (payment processing). We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your records are kept while your account is active. You can delete pets, documents, and other records in the App, and you can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted transport and Google Cloud infrastructure, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

PawVault is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
